Changes: LocalGuard imports only unconditional and third-party domain-wide
blocking filters; those are restricted to third-party non-navigation requests.
Compatible network exceptions are translated to browser allow rules. Filters
requiring unsupported blocking syntax are omitted. First-party exceptions
are unnecessary and omitted. An unsupported third-party exception rejects
the candidate update. Rules are grouped by destination domains.
